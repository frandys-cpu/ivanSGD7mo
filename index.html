TECHNOLOGY & INNOVATION

Smart Panama

Innovation That Transforms Our Country

Have you ever wondered some of the uses of technology in Panama in its various sectors?

Technology is changing the way we live, work and connect in Panama. From education to entertainment, from enterprises and, more importantly, the Panama Canal, innovation helps our country grow and move forward.

Some of the sectors where technology is used

Education

Education in Panama has moved from traditional chalkboard-based learning toward a more modern, interactive and technology-driven approach. Computers, digital platforms and online resources help students learn in new ways.

Entertainment

Entertainment in Panama is being transformed by digital platforms like X and TikTok and the rise of video games. People can share videos, communicate, stream content and play online.

Enterprises

Businesses use technology to communicate with customers, advertise their products, manage information and offer online services. These tools can improve productivity and help companies grow.

Panama Canal

The Panama Canal is a major international trade route. Modern communication, navigation, monitoring and traffic-management systems help support its operations and safety.

Explore the Panama Canal

The Panama Canal connects the Atlantic and Pacific oceans. Technology helps ships cross it safely and efficiently.

💡 Did You Know?

The Panama Canal can use drones for certain inspection, monitoring, and security-related tasks. These technologies help support the monitoring of important infrastructure.

🌎 Fun Fact!

Panama helps tourists travel more easily through the acceptance of credit cards in many places, GPS navigation, and ridesharing apps like Uber.
